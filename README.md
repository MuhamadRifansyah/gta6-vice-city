Solmar Bay is a full 3D open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser, built on three.js. The city is alive: traffic flows down the streets, pedestrians walk the sidewalks, and the police react to everything you do. Jack a car off the street, build up a wanted level, and lose the cops — or fight back with a pistol. Five story missions take you across the city, with a HUD, a live minimap, save files, and sound effects generated on the fly. Everything in the game — the city, the story, the art — is original work made for this entry; nothing is taken from Rockstar.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Play it

Node 22 or later, nothing to install:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.

## Controls

- **W A S D** — drive
- **E** — steal a car
- **Mouse** — aim and shoot
- **Q** — draw the pistol
- **R** — reload
- **P** — pause
- **M** — mute
